Conectafam Plus

Conectafam Plus is a production web platform for a multilevel network in Spain. People join by invitation and work inside that hierarchy: training content for their teams, boards for files and pages, landing pages, contacts, and WhatsApp templates.

I built and operate the Django application, including access rules for different user types and image and video pipelines (WebP and HLS). It is in production with more than 1,700 registered users.
