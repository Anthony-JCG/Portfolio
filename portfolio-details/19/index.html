Algorithmic trading strategy using reinforcement learning

IT engineering thesis (2025) at Universidad Estatal Península de Santa Elena. I built a PPO agent for Ethereum futures on Binance and compared it with a rules-based strategy that used technical indicators to open and close trades.

The agent was trained on 5-minute candles from October 2021 to September 2023 and evaluated on the following year (October 2023–September 2024). In that backtest, net ROI was 66.3% for the baseline and 82.8% for the agent. I also ran it on a live Binance account through the API, as a demonstration that it could execute live orders; that phase was not used to measure results.
